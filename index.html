Look up any Canadian license plate

PL8 PEEK is a mobile app that turns a Canadian license plate number into the vehicle's make, model, year, VIN, and full specifications — in seconds. Enter a plate and province, and get accurate vehicle details pulled from licensed registration and government VIN data.

What you get

  • Make, model, and year
  • VIN (Vehicle Identification Number)
  • Trim, engine, body type, drivetrain, and fuel type
  • A private history of your past lookups
